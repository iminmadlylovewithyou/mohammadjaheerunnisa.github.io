Because in a world where everyone is trying so hard to be noticed, she somehow leaves an impact just by being herself.

She doesn’t force kindness.

She doesn’t perform innocence.

She doesn’t act soft.

She simply is.

And that is rare.

There’s also this quiet hope around her that’s difficult to explain.

The kind of presence that makes people feel lighter without even realising why.

“Not in a loud way…
just in a quiet, still way.”

I think some people are beautiful because of their face.

But some people become beautiful because of the peace their existence gives to others.

She is the second kind.

And honestly… I hope life stays gentle with her.

Because people with hearts like hers deserve softness, happiness, and people who truly understand how rare they are.

Dedicated to MOHAMMAD JAHEERUNNISA
